## Shared-library rules

Reuse canonical concept IDs, not duplicate lessons. Begin with GK, reasoning, maths and English; add Hindi, Punjabi, Punjab GK, computer and pedagogy when officially relevant. Match depth, language and syllabus version before making questions eligible for another exam.

Every exam mapping retains source URL, notification/year, page/heading, subject, unit, topic, subtopic and concept ID. Label editorial expansions separately from official wording. Separate static GK, state GK and dated current affairs.

Content labels: VERIFIED / AI-GENERATED (needs human review) / MISSING. Track HI/PA/EN independently, with reviewer and copyright/source evidence. Translation presence is not proof of translation quality.

Questions require stable IDs, duplicate fingerprints, concept tags, difficulty and origin. Verified PYQs additionally require original exam, year, shift/paper, source and final answer key. Otherwise label UNVERIFIED and exclude from verified counts. Similar-state questions retain their original exam identity.

Mocks must follow their own official allocation, section choices, timing, qualifying rules and negative marking. Count unique eligible questions; overlapping topic pools are not extra inventory.
