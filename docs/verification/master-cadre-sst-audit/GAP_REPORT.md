# Punjab Master Cadre SST — History: Steps 1 and 2

Audit date: 10 October 2026. Baseline: **2022 recruitment, 4161 posts, Social Science/SST**. No notification year was supplied in this input, so 2022 is the explicitly selected archival baseline, not a claim about the next recruitment. This report audits History in depth, inventories the other SST domains, and stops before generating teaching content or making application changes.

## Outcome

The current application has a much larger curriculum than the earlier live version, but **complete official History coverage is not established**. All 16 registered History topic routes resolve to lessons; that is 100% route availability, not 100% syllabus coverage. The actual main lesson text leaves several explicit official headings absent or shallow. False-confidence labels and unverified historical question claims are the most urgent issues.

Official History has **21 numbered syllabus entries** across Punjab (6), India (13) and World (2). The application blueprint collapses them into three umbrella labels, hiding requirements such as Harsha, AD 700–1200 kingdoms and colonial education. The audit preserves all 21 entries and supplies a review-required expansion to 238 concept candidates, plus 22 candidates for four specifically requested extensions. These 260 candidates are an audit outline, not a claim that a government supplied an exhaustive atomic syllabus.

### What is in scope, and what is supplemental

- Ancient, medieval and modern India and national movement: explicitly present through the thirteen India entries below.
- Punjab: ten Gurus, Banda, Misls, Ranjit Singh, Anglo-Sikh relations/wars and annexation explicitly listed.
- Colonial Punjab, Singh Sabha, Akali and Partition are retained as requested extensions and national-movement context. They are **not separately named in the six official Punjab headings**. Do not relabel them as explicit government headings without further evidence.
- World: Renaissance/Reformation and the two World Wars explicitly listed. French/Russian revolutions, Cold War and decolonisation can support context, but must not displace the actual listed lessons.
- Polity, Geography and Economics are full alternative SST sections, not automatically extra compulsory components of History. A candidate choosing History must choose a second section; no second section is assumed in this audit.
- Pedagogy is not listed as a separate section in this recruitment syllabus. PSTET eligibility is a different exam. General English, general Punjabi, generic GK and Reasoning are not separate scored sections in the inspected SST syllabus. Keep common-library modules available as optional support; do not silently include them in History mocks.

### Method and limits

Imported the current working tree through the existing TypeScript transpilation approach, then called `getExamById`, `getLessonByTopicId` and the exam-scoped question engine. No application data regenerated. Local source snapshot HEAD `a7fc514` plus substantial uncommitted content changes. Matrix describes those changes, not an assumed deployed release.

- Complete = full required depth, correct facts, meaningful summaries/cards, relevant sufficient reviewed MCQs with option explanations, verified PYQs/resources and translation parity. **None is certified Complete.**
- Partial = draft material/candidate evidence exists but does not meet that standard. A `complete` self-label is not accepted as evidence.
- Missing = no linked field or no candidate concept evidence found at the inspected level; content elsewhere may still discuss it.
- For **concept** rows, explicit concept IDs/evidence mappings are absent from the application. `CONCEPT_COVERAGE.csv` records provisional anchor matches from the candidate linked lessons and English question stems, along with exact lesson/question IDs. Matches retrieve candidates only; a token occurrence is not proof of a full explanation or a correct relevant MCQ. Every such positive is Partial. HI/PA positives mean parent draft exists, not concept translation parity. A negative needs a human semantic pass before declaring no discussion anywhere.
- Topic-level **In mock** means eligible in the current engine, not a section-balanced official paper. **PYQ** means independently source-verified; all rows remain Missing despite self-labelled entries.
- Resources are Partial for available references/search portals; video playback, exact topic relevance, free availability, language and reuse rights are not certified.
